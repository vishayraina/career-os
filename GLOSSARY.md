# Career OS

A lightweight agentic career operating system that prioritizes daily career actions and manages opportunities and relationships.

## Language

**Opportunity**:
A specific employment position at a target company tracked through its entire lifecycle from initial discovery to final outcome.
_Avoid_: Job posting, lead, listing, vacancy

**Application**:
The active submission state and metadata of an Opportunity once formally pursued or submitted.
_Avoid_: Job application (as a separate entity), submission, candidature

**Person**:
An individual human in the user's professional network, such as a recruiter, hiring manager, referrer, or peer engineer.
_Avoid_: Contact, connection, lead, user

**Pipeline**:
The unified stage progression an Opportunity moves through (Discovered → Considering → Applied → Screening → Technical → Offer → Closed/Rejected).
_Avoid_: Funnel, hiring track

**Daily Career Brief**:
The concise daily briefing document synthesizing prioritized actions, new opportunities, and relationship follow-ups.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, status digest, morning report

**Action**:
A discrete, high-leverage task recommended in the Daily Career Brief for human execution.
_Avoid_: Task, todo, recommendation

**Relationship Status**:
The qualitative warmth and engagement level between the user and a Person (Cold, Aware, Warm, Engaged, Advocate).
_Avoid_: Connection tier, stage

**Fit Score**:
The assessed alignment between an Opportunity and the user's career profile and constraints.
_Avoid_: Match percentage, ranking

**Skip Item**:
A low-leverage opportunity or deferred task explicitly filtered out of the day's brief to protect user focus.
_Avoid_: Backlog item, trash, archive

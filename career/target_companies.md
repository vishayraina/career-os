# Dynamic Discovery Rule

The scout must NOT limit itself to this file.

Every search cycle should also discover:
1. New AI startups
2. Newly funded AI companies
3. Companies hiring heavily for AI/ML
4. Companies building foundation-model infrastructure
5. Companies building speech/audio/voice products
6. Companies building multimodal systems
7. Companies building inference infrastructure
8. Companies with unusually strong Senior/Staff/Principal AI opportunities
9. Companies offering international relocation or visa sponsorship
10. Companies where Vishay's ASR + ML systems background provides an unusual advantage

A company should be added to the universe when it repeatedly produces relevant opportunities.

# Geographic Search Rule

Search globally rather than assuming the company has a role in every location.

For each opportunity record:
- Company
- Role
- Level
- Team
- Location
- Remote eligibility
- Visa sponsorship signal
- Relocation support
- Compensation if available
- Posting date
- Source URL
- Fit category
- Why Vishay is relevant
- Missing skills / stretch requirements

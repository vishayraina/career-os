# Target Geographies

Search globally rather than assuming the company has a role in every location.

## Primary Target Geographies

### 1. United States
- San Francisco Bay Area, New York City, Seattle, or US-Remote with visa sponsorship (O-1, H-1B transfer).

### 2. United Kingdom
- **Primary Cities**: London, Cambridge.
- Specialized focus on AI, speech, multimodal, and research-engineering roles.

### 3. France
- **Primary City**: Paris.
- Major target for foundation-model, applied AI, and research-engineering opportunities.

### 4. Switzerland
- **Primary Cities**: Zurich, Lausanne, Geneva.

### 5. Netherlands
- **Primary Cities**: Amsterdam, Eindhoven, Delft.
- Particular focus where ML intersects with systems, optimization, computer vision, hardware, and industrial AI (e.g. ASML, NXP).

### 6. Germany
- **Primary Cities**: Berlin, Munich, Stuttgart, Hamburg, Frankfurt.
- High relevance for both dedicated AI startups and ML systems engineering inside major industrial/enterprise leaders.

### 7. Canada
- **Primary Cities**: Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Waterloo, Edmonton.
- Deep AI research ecosystem (Vector, Mila, Cohere, etc.).

### 8. Singapore
- Serious target geography covering global tech, high-growth Southeast Asian tech leaders, and tier-1 financial/enterprise AI institutions.

### 9. United Arab Emirates (UAE)
- **Primary Emirates**: Dubai, Abu Dhabi.
- Substantial AI hiring across frontier/government-backed ecosystems (G42, TII, AI71, Presight, MBZUAI) and regional tech leaders.

### 10. Ireland
- **Primary City**: Dublin.

### 11. Japan
- **Primary Cities**: Tokyo, Osaka.

### 12. Australia
- **Primary Cities**: Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane.

---

## Global Search Rules
- Search globally for remote-friendly or relocation-sponsored roles across these markets.
- Always check visa sponsorship signals and relocation support.

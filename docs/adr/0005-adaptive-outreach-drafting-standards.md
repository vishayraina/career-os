# Adaptive Outreach Drafting Standards

To ensure external networking messages maintain high conversion rates without sounding artificial or triggering LinkedIn character limits, the Network Manager generates channel-adaptive drafts: strict under-300-character notes for LinkedIn connection requests, and 100–150 word technical peer-to-peer drafts for emails and follow-ups. All drafts strictly prohibit sycophancy or generic filler, focusing directly on technical overlap and low-friction calls to action.
